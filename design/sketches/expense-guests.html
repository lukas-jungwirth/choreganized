Guests: sharing a cost with someone outside the household

A guest is a name in the tally and nothing more: no account, no chores, no lists. You add one at the moment you split a cost with them, and you can send them a link to a page that shows their share and only their share.

The example: a weekend in Südtirol with Jonas. Lukas paid the hut and a dinner, Elisabeth the groceries, Jonas the fuel.

Your two questions

  • How do I add a new person, only in the spendings tab?Yes, only in Expenses, where you need them."Add a guest" is the last row of the split sheet, and "Someone else…" is the last entry of the "who paid" picker. Both open the same one-field sheet. Guests are managed under Expense settings. They never show under Members.
  • How can this person see their share?Through a private link, read only.No account and no household of their own. The page shows what they owe or are owed, who to pay, and the expenses they are part of. It works the same whether or not they use Haushaltsheld themselves.

What the guest sees

A page outside the app, opened from the link. Nothing to sign in to and nothing to change. It follows the phone's language and its light or dark setting.

The rules behind it

  • A guest is only in what you tick them into.Never part of the default split, so the weekly shop stays between the household. The "Include in new expenses" switch is the one exception, and it is yours to flip.
  • A guest can pay, too."Jonas paid €132.00 for fuel" is logged by one of you with Jonas as the payer. Guests cannot add or change anything themselves.
  • Who pays whom counts everyone.The app keeps the fewest payments across members and guests. It can therefore say "Jonas pays Elisabeth" even if Jonas only shared costs with Lukas. The sums are right either way.
  • The link is the key.Long and unguessable, one per guest. Anyone who has it can read that guest's page, so it can be replaced at any time. Search engines are told to stay away.
  • Removing keeps the history.A settled guest can be removed: gone from the pickers, the link off, the old expenses still readable with their name.
  • Not in this plan.Linking two Haushaltsheld households, trips as their own group, guests adding expenses, several currencies.
