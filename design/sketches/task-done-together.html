Done together, and who did it

Ticking a task stays one tap and still means "I did it". The pop-up that already offers Undo gets one new row of faces. Tap a face to add a housemate, tap your own to take yourself out. That one row covers done together, done by somebody else, and your own task done by your partner.

The phones work. Tap the faces and watch the points, the next turn and the standings follow.

Your questions

  • A step in between, or adjust it in the pop-up?Adjust it in the pop-up.Most ticks are your own task done by you. A question before every tick would cost a tap each time to answer what the app already guessed right. In the pop-up the right guess costs nothing and a wrong one costs one tap.
  • Should I be able to tick my own task for somebody else?Yes, and it comes for free.Elisabeth cleaned the bathroom on your turn and you tick it: tap her face, tap yours off. No separate feature, the same row.
  • What about the "Who did it?" question on other people's tasks?It stays, unchanged.On somebody else's task the app has no good guess, and a silent wrong credit is the worse mistake. The question picks one person and the pop-up opens with that face on. It gets no "together" answer: sharing is always added in the pop-up.
  • Does it work from the Home card?Yes, unchanged.Home already opens this same pop-up after "Mark done". The row arrives there without Home changing at all.
  • One "Assign to" row per housemate is too many.Agreed. One row, then pick.A household can have five members, which makes four rows today. With three or more people it becomes one "Reassign" row that opens a short list. With two people it stays the direct one-tap row.

The pop-up after a tick

Same pop-up as today plus the "Who did it?" row. Each tap saves at once, there is no confirm button. Undo still takes the whole thing back.

Reassign: one row, then pick

Shown for a household of five. The task sheet loses three rows, and "Anyone" becomes reachable from here, which it is not today.

What was decided

  • The question before the tick stays.Only for other people's tasks, with its two answers, as today. Your own and "Anyone" tasks go straight to the pop-up.
  • Rounding to the nearest whole.20 by 3 gives 7 each, so 21 in total. Rounding down would make sharing cost points.
  • The standings line shows the gap."You're 5 points ahead of Elisabeth", not the two totals.
  • No correcting later.Once the pop-up is closed, the credit stands. If that bites, the fix is the same row of faces behind a tap on the History line.
