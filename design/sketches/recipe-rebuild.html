A recipe the app understands, so you only check what it couldn't

Three things change underneath. A recipe has parts (Bolognese, Béchamel). An ingredient is a name plus a note, so the list gets "Faschiertes" and not "Hackfleisch, gemischtes". A step stores which words name which ingredient, so "Zwiebelwürfel" is the onion because the import or you said so, not because a matcher guessed.

The sample is the Chefkoch lasagne. Parts of the sketch work: settle the checks in R1, tick ingredients in C1, tap words in E3.
