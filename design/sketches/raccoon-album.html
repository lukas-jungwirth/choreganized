What unlocks one

  • Home actually rendered it for you (per member, not per household — you each fill your own album).
  • A raccoon you haven't met is only a silhouette and a question mark. No hints.
  • Number order = album order: everyday → winter → summer → holidays.

Rarity, three steps

  • ★ All year: the everyday poses that show in every season.
  • ★★ Seasonal: everyday poses tied to some seasons. The chip gives the months.
  • ★★★ Special days: holidays and your birthday, with the holo frame.
  • Fun stats stay fixed per raccoon (1–5 dots); first met and times seen are live.

Copy lives in the catalog

  • Name, title and fun fact per raccoon in en.ts / de.ts — Austrian puns welcome in German.
  • Stars come from the pose itself (its seasons, or being a special day), so new art needs no extra field. Stats sit next to the pose in mascots.json.
