Choreganized · Household app

A calm home for your household

A mobile-first web app (installable as a PWA) for Lukas & Elisabeth. Four tabs — Home, Shopping, Cooking, Tasks — no finances, no message board. Clean, warm, minimal: a soft paper background, one sage green for actions, a terracotta for people & points. Fraunces for headings, Figtree for everything else. Newly renamed to Choreganized — pick a logo below and I'll roll it into the mockups.
